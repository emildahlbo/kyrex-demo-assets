# PULSE (fictional demo brand)

- `pulse-one-v4.glb`: the Pulse One headphones, modelled in Blender after the Pulse One product photo
  (theme-core `demo-stores/pulse/model_pulse_one.py`), quantised with glTF-Transform. Own work, no third-party assets.
- `pulse-one-studio-v4.png`: studio render of that model in the product photo's view (product image).
- `pulse-one-poster-v4.png`: render of the model as the 3D hero opens (still image for the hero).
